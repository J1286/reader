Neverleaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Neverleaf is a personal reading library that lets you import and read files on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information stored on your device

Books and documents you import, library and folder details, reading positions and progress, and app preferences may be stored in your browser or device storage. This information is used to provide the app's library, reading, and settings features. Neverleaf does not require you to create an account.

2. Imported files, exports, and backups

You choose which files to import. The app provides options to export or back up information, which may create files on your device or copy text to your clipboard. You decide whether to save, transfer, or share those files or text. Any copies you share or store outside the app are subject to the practices of the relevant app or service.

3. Network requests and third-party libraries

Neverleaf is designed to keep your library and reading data in local device storage rather than uploading them to a developer-operated account or synchronization service. However, the app may make network requests to load third-party resources used for icons and document processing, and to check for app version information hosted with the app. Your device's IP address and standard request or browser information may be visible to the relevant hosting or resource providers when those requests occur. Their handling of such information is governed by their own policies.

Some features may require an internet connection to retrieve resources or check for updates. Previously loaded app resources may be cached by your browser or device for offline use, but offline availability is not guaranteed in every situation.

4. Sharing, advertising, and sale of information

Neverleaf does not include an app feature for sending your library or reading progress to the developer, and it is not designed to sell your personal information or serve targeted advertising. Network requests to third-party resource providers are described above. If you choose to share an export, the recipient and services you use to share it may process that information independently.

5. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app or browser's site data, or the operating system or browser removes it. You can remove books through the app's available controls. Clearing site data or uninstalling/resetting the app environment may also remove your library, settings, and reading progress. Exported files and backups are separate copies; you are responsible for deleting them from locations where you saved or shared them.

6. Children's privacy

Neverleaf is a general-purpose reading app and is not specifically designed for children. It does not require an account or ask users to provide personal information to use its core reading features.

7. Security

Keeping library information in local storage reduces the need to transmit it to the developer, but no storage method or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and any exported backups.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its dependencies, or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the revision date shown above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or Neverleaf's privacy practices, contact neverleaf.app@gmail.com.
